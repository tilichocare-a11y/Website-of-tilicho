Our story

Nepal's own cleaning brand.

Our name comes from Tilicho Lake in Manang, one of the highest lakes in the world, with clear blue water beneath the snow. That feeling of pure, fresh clean is what we want for every Nepali home.

We're starting with simple products that make the hardest jobs easier. Our goal is to build Nepal's complete range of cleaning products, from the bathroom to the kitchen to the windows.

How can I pay?

We'll confirm the payment options for your location when you place your order on WhatsApp.

How long does a pack last?

A toilet wand box has 16 heads and usually lasts about 6–7 weeks for a normal household. A drain stick pack has 12 sticks. Replace the stick in each drain every 3 days.

Are the heads safe for my toilet and pipes?

The heads are made to scrub your toilet, not to go down it. Please do not flush them. Press the button to drop the used head into a bin. Flushing heads can block pipes and septic tanks.

How does the drain stick work?

Drop one stick into the drain, then run a little water or leave it to work. It helps clean and deodorise the pipe and keep the drain flowing. Replace it every 3 days. Keep away from children, avoid contact with eyes, and do not mix with other cleaners.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the product and the packaging, and we'll arrange a replacement. See our Return & Refund Policy.
